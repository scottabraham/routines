$30 billion annualized run rate vs OpenAI's $25-27 billion. The company most people still call "the other AI startup" is now the top earner in the game.

I keep thinking about what this actually signals.

OpenAI had the brand. The head start. The Microsoft billions. The household name. And a smaller team in San Francisco quietly built a better business.

This isn't just a revenue story — it's a distribution story. Anthropic bet hard on enterprise and developer tools while OpenAI chased consumer attention. Turns out, selling to companies that pay real money beats viral chatbot moments.

The AI race isn't going to be won by whoever has the flashiest demo. It's going to be won by whoever builds the thing people can't stop paying for.

If you're building in this space — or betting your career on it — pay attention to where the money actually flows. Not the headlines.
