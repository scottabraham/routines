# LinkedIn Post — June 25, 2026

OpenAI just built its own chip. Took them 9 months.

They're calling it Jalapeno (yes, really). Co-developed with Broadcom, it's purpose-built for inference — running AI models, not training them. The headline number: 50% cheaper than Nvidia GPUs for the same workload.

Nine months from first design to tape-out. That might be the fastest high-performance ASIC development cycle in semiconductor history.

Here's what I think matters most: OpenAI isn't coming for Nvidia's training business. They're going after the runtime costs. Every ChatGPT query, every API call, every Codex autocomplete — that's inference, and at OpenAI's scale, it's where the real money burns.

Cut that cost in half and the margins on a $15B+ revenue business change overnight. The "AI companies can't make money" crowd just lost their best talking point.

We're watching the AI stack consolidate in real time. The companies building the models now want to own the silicon running them too.

How long before every major AI lab has its own chip program?
