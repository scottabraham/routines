# LinkedIn Post — July 1, 2026

Oracle just had its worst week since the 2001 dot-com bust.

Stock down 19% in five trading days. Down 55% from the ~$900B market cap it hit back in September.

Here's what should worry every software company, not just Oracle: they're not bleeding because AI failed. They're bleeding because they bet everything on AI succeeding, and the bill came due.

$130B in debt. Capex up 162% this year to $56B, chasing data center buildout. Negative free cash flow of $24B. And now they need to raise another $40B in FY2027 just to keep the lights on.

Meanwhile investors are asking the scarier question: if AI models can just do the software, why do you still need the company selling it? Software stocks broadly are down 16% this year on that exact fear.

I've spent the last couple years thinking the AI race was "who builds the best model." Turns out the real race might be "who can afford to keep building" — and "does anyone still need what you built."

Oracle isn't the outlier here. It's the preview.

If your career or your company's roadmap assumes SaaS is safe ground because AI can't touch it, this week's a good time to double-check that.

Bubble, correction, or just the bill finally coming due — what's your read?
