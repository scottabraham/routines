# LinkedIn Post — June 18, 2026

The White House gave Anthropic 90 minutes to kill its most powerful AI models.

Not a week. Not a hearing. Ninety minutes.

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick sent an export-control directive barring Fable 5 and Mythos 5 from use "by any foreign national" — inside or outside the U.S. — including Anthropic's own non-citizen employees. Since there's no realistic way to wall off those users, Anthropic pulled the models for everyone.

Three days. That's how long the public had access before the government flipped the switch.

Here's the part that should concern every builder in tech:

The cybersecurity community largely disputes the national security claims behind the order. Other publicly available models have similar capabilities. This wasn't a measured policy response — it was a trust breakdown between one company and one administration, and millions of users paid the price.

We've entered the era where your AI provider's relationship with Washington matters more than its benchmark scores.

Anthropic says it expects to restore access. But the precedent is already set — any frontier model can be pulled overnight, for reasons that may have nothing to do with the technology itself.

How comfortable are you building on a platform the government can shut down in 90 minutes?
