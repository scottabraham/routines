# LinkedIn Post — June 24, 2026

Oracle just cut 21,000 jobs in a single year and said the quiet part out loud: AI did it.

Not "restructuring for efficiency." Not "market conditions." Their actual SEC filing says AI adoption resulted in workforce reductions — and more are coming.

That's 13% of the company. Gone. $1.84 billion in severance, nearly 4x what they spent on layoffs last year.

Here's what makes this different from every other tech layoff headline: Oracle isn't struggling. Revenue is up. They're spending billions on AI data centers for OpenAI and Meta. Business is booming — they just need fewer humans to run it.

I think this is the moment we stop debating "will AI replace jobs?" and start asking "how fast?"

Because if a company will publicly tell the SEC that AI is why they cut a fifth of their workforce — while posting record numbers — every other boardroom is doing the same math quietly.

The playbook isn't to panic. It's to make sure you're on the side of the equation that's building with AI, not getting replaced by it.

What's your company saying internally about AI and headcount? I'd bet it's more than they're saying publicly.
