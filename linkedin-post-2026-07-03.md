# LinkedIn Post — July 3, 2026

Entry-level jobs didn't disappear. They got promoted without a raise.

That's the real story buried in PwC's 2026 AI Jobs Barometer: in the most AI-exposed fields, "entry-level" openings are now 7x more likely to demand skills we used to call senior — leadership, judgment, critical thinking. 52% of the new skills required are senior-level, versus just 7% in less AI-exposed jobs.

Meanwhile, June's jobs report landed at 57,000 new jobs — less than half the 110,000 economists expected.

So AI isn't deleting the bottom rung of the ladder. It's raising it. Openings for these "leveled-up" entry roles grew 35% since 2019, while classic entry-level openings shrank 10%.

Which means the on-the-job learning that used to turn a 22-year-old into a professional is quietly getting outsourced to AI — before day one.

If you're hiring: are you asking juniors to show up senior, then wondering why nobody junior ever gets hired?

If you're graduating this year: what are you doing to build the judgment AI can't fake?
