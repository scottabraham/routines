# LinkedIn Post — September 14, 2026

Three rivals just agreed on something. That never happens.

Dario Amodei, Sam Altman and Elon Musk — three guys who'd probably fight over who invented the wheel — all came out this week saying the same thing: AI is moving too fast, and maybe it's time to slow down.

OpenAI even shelved its IPO plans over it.

I've been writing software since 2001. Back then the scariest thing in tech was Internet Explorer 6 mangling your CSS. Now the people building the most powerful models on earth are the ones tapping the brakes.

That's not nothing.

I keep thinking about a codebase I worked on years ago. We shipped fast, cut corners, told ourselves we'd clean it up later. We never did. Cost us months, eventually.

Speed feels great right up until it doesn't.

Maybe that's what's happening here, just at a much bigger scale — with the actual inventors admitting it out loud instead of quietly patching it in a sprint.

Does it change anything when the people racing each other are the ones asking to slow the race down? Or is this just good timing on the PR front?

Genuinely curious what others think.
