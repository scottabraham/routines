# Scott's Voice — LinkedIn Writing Style Guide

## Tone
- Conversational and direct — write like you're talking to a smart friend over coffee
- Confident but not arrogant — share opinions backed by observation, not hype
- Curious and forward-looking — ask "what does this mean for us?" not just "what happened"
- Real talk over corporate speak — no buzzword salads, no empty jargon

## Structure
- Hook with a bold take or surprising fact in the first line
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max)
- Use line breaks generously for readability on mobile
- End with a question or call-to-action that invites genuine conversation

## Do
- Use "I" and share personal perspective
- Reference real numbers, names, and specifics
- Connect tech news to broader career/industry implications
- Use occasional humor or a sharp observation
- Keep it under 200 words — respect people's feed

## Don't
- Use hashtag walls (#innovation #AI #disruption #leadership)
- Write clickbait or "I'm humbled to announce" energy
- Over-explain things the audience already knows
- Be preachy or lecture-y
- Use emojis excessively — one or two max if any
